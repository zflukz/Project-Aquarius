\subsection{Project introduction \& problem statement} 

\tab Floods are one of the most dangerous natural disasters, causing significant loss of life,
property, and environmental resources. After a flood, rescue teams must locate people who may
be stranded in isolated or dangerous areas. However, post-flood environments can be difficult to
access due to damaged roads, strong currents, debris, unstable structures, and limited visibility.
These conditions increase the risk to rescue personnel and can make conventional ground-based
search operations slow and difficult. \newline
\tab Recent studies have demonstrated the potential of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) to
support search and rescue operations by providing aerial observation over large and difficult-toaccess areas. UAVs can collect information from above without requiring rescue personnel to
immediately enter hazardous areas. Research on UAV-based disaster monitoring and cooperative
navigation has also demonstrated that UAVs can be used to improve area coverage and provide
information about disaster-affected environments. \newline
\tab Human detection is an important part of search and rescue operations. However, detecting
people manually from drone footage can be difficult and time-consuming, especially when
monitoring large post-flood areas. In addition, factors such as low-light conditions, weather,
complex backgrounds, and visual obstructions may affect the ability of rescue personnel to
identify humans. Therefore, the camera mounted on the UAV can be used to capture images or
video from the affected area, while image processing and AI-based human detection techniques
can support the automatic identification of people from the captured visual data. This can reduce
the need for continuous manual observation and help rescue personnel identify possible human
locations more efficiently. \newline
\tab However, detecting a person alone is not sufficient for rescue operations. Rescue
personnel also need to know where the detected person is located so that the information
can be used for further rescue planning. Therefore, the detected human position can be
estimated using the UAV's sensor and positioning information and then transmitted to a ground
station or rescue personnel in real time. \newline
\tab Therefore, our team aims to develop a UAV-based system that combines AI-based human
detection, thermal/visual sensing, localization, and wireless communication to assist rescue
teams in post-flood environments. The primary objective of the system is to detect humans,
determine and mark their locations, and transmit the detected information back to rescue
personnel. By providing this information from a safe distance, the system can reduce the need
for rescuers to immediately enter hazardous areas and support them in making safer and more
effective decisions during flood response operations. At this stage, the system focuses on human
detection and location reporting, rather than autonomous path planning or approaching the
detected person.

\subsection{Objectives}



\begin{enumerate}

    \item To develop an AI-based system capable of
    detecting humans from UAV camera data.

    \item To estimate and report the location of detected
    humans using UAV sensor information.

    \item To develop a system capable of processing
    detection information efficiently.

    \item To provide a monitoring interface for displaying
    UAV and human detection information.

    \item To evaluate the performance of the proposed
    human detection and localization system.

\end{enumerate}


% =================================================
% 1.2 Scope
% =================================================

\subsection{Scope}

The scope of this project covers the development of a
UAV-based human detection and localization system. The
system consists of UAV hardware, camera and sensor
components, an AI-based detection module, and a monitoring
system.
\subsubsection{Solutions} 

The system focuses on detecting possible humans within
the UAV camera field of view and estimating their
locations based on available sensor and positioning data.


% =================================================
% 1.3 Expected Results
% =================================================

\hspace{2cm}\subsection{Expected Results}

The expected result of this project is a prototype system
that can detect possible humans from UAV camera data and
provide their estimated locations to an operator.

The system is expected to support search and rescue
operations by reducing the time required to identify
possible human locations and providing useful information
for further investigation.
